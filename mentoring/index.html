Mentoring

20 undergraduate researchers from 9 institutions · 3 master’s students · 11 K–12 teachers

Mentoring is one of my favorite parts of research. I work with undergraduates, master's students, and K-12 teachers and I try to give every mentee ownership of a question they care about. Fourteen of my mentees have co-authored published or submitted work. One community-college mentee transferred to NC State and stayed on the project, and two mentees have begun Ph.D. programs. In Summer 2026, seven undergraduate developers (two team leads and five developers) and nine K-12 teachers took part in ExLAIM, an ExLENT-funded program I lead, co-designing classroom tools for MerryQuery.
