Hello! I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at North Carolina State University (expected May 2027), working with [Dr. Tiffany Barnes](https://eliza.csc.ncsu.edu) and [Dr. DK Xu](https://dongkuanx27.github.io/) in the Game2Learn and GIC labs.

My dissertation, **Toward Human-AI Co-Intelligence in Computing Education**, studies how students, developers, and educators actually collaborate with AI systems, and how to design those systems to be trustworthy, adaptive, and pedagogically grounded. I combine mixed-methods and human-centered research with educational data mining to understand where learners struggle, then build and evaluate tools that help. The same questions carry into health communication, where responsible AI design matters just as much.

So far this work has produced 16 peer-reviewed publications, a [Best Technical Demonstration Award at AAAI 2025](https://aaai.org/about-aaai/aaai-awards/aaai-25-paper-awards/) for MerryQuery, and a [Best Research Paper Award at IEEE VL/HCC 2024](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10714525) for Jigsaw.

### What I'm building

- **[MerryQuery](https://exploremq.benyamintabarsi.com/):** A GenAI-powered educational assistant that grounds its answers in course materials and instructor preferences. Deployed across three semesters of an introductory MATLAB course reaching 750+ students, and used as a development platform in a graduate education course.
- **HelpLens:** An eight-dimension, theory-informed framework and annotated dataset for comparing how teaching staff and LLMs answer students' programming help requests, applied to 172 paired responses.
- **SafeTalk:** An expert-informed AI coaching system that helps parents rehearse sensitive health conversations with their teens, adapting coaching principles to a high-stakes, nonprofessional setting.
- **ExLAIM:** An ExLENT-funded program I lead and coordinate that pairs student developers with K-12 teachers to co-design classroom AI tools.
- **[LLMs in Classrooms](https://go.ncsu.edu/chatgpt-workshop):** Professional development that prepares K-12 educators to integrate generative AI into their teaching.

I also care a lot about **mentoring and teaching**: I have guided 20 undergraduate researchers, 3 master's students, and 11 K-12 teachers, and I teach introductory computing as a Preparing the Professoriate and Mentored Teaching Fellow.

I am always eager to collaborate with researchers building tools that make learning easier and more effective. If you work on educational technology, human-centered AI, or computing education, feel free to reach out!
